Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

PricePilot collects data necessary to provide tax-aware pricing optimization and performance analytics.

  • Shopify Data: If you use our Shopify App, we collect your shop URL, email address, and basic theme information to enable script injection.
  • Usage Data: We collect request metadata (country, currency, base price) to calculate optimized prices. We do NOT collect personally identifiable information (PII) from your end-customers.
  • Account Data: Email addresses and hashed API keys for authentication.

2. How We Use Your Data

Merchant Privacy: Your pricing logic and store data are private to your account. We never sell your data to third parties.
  • Executing real-time price optimizations.
  • Preventing unauthorized domain usage and API abuse.
  • Providing conversion and revenue lift analytics in your dashboard.

3. Data Retention & Shopify Compliance

We retain your data as long as your account is active. If you uninstall the PricePilot Shopify App, we delete your shop's session data within 48 hours in compliance with Shopify's data protection requirements.

4. Security

All data is encrypted in transit via TLS and at rest within our secure Supabase/PostgreSQL infrastructure. API keys are hashed using industry-standard algorithms.

5. Contact Us

For questions about this policy, contact us at support@pricepilot.site.
