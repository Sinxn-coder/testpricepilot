# PricePilot Quick-Start Guide 🚀

Welcome to PricePilot! Follow these 3 simple steps to start optimizing your store's pricing today.

## 1. Connect Your Account
1. Log in to the [PricePilot Dashboard](https://pricepilot.site/dashboard).
2. Go to the **Access** tab.
3. Click **Generate API Key** and copy it.

## 2. Install the Plugin
### **For Shopify:**
1. Install the PricePilot App.
2. Paste your API Key into the app settings inside Shopify.
3. Go to **Online Store > Themes > Customize**.
4. In **App Embeds**, enable "PricePilot Optimizer" and click **Save**.

### **For WooCommerce:**
1. Download the PricePilot plugin and upload it to your WordPress site.
2. Go to **Settings > PricePilot**.
3. Paste your API Key and click **Save**.

## 3. Verify & Monitor
1. Visit any product page on your store.
2. If PricePilot is working, you will see a subtle fade animation as prices are optimized (e.g., $10.00 becomes $9.99).
3. Check your **Analytics** tab in the PricePilot dashboard to see your revenue lift in real-time.

---

## ❓ Troubleshooting & FAQ

**Q: Why aren't my prices changing?**
- Ensure you have enabled the "App Embed" in your Shopify theme settings.
- Check that your API key is correctly entered.
- Verify that your store domain is listed in the "Domain Registry" on your dashboard.

**Q: Does PricePilot affect page speed?**
- No. PricePilot uses a lightweight, asynchronous script (< 5KB) that loads after your main content, ensuring zero impact on your SEO or user experience.
